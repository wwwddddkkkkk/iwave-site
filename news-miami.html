EVENT · June 13, 2025

IWAVE at Miami International Boat Show — making waves with the world's first water go-kart.

SHARE Twitter LinkedIn Email

IWAVE made a splash at this year's Miami International Boat Show — one of the world's premier showcases for innovation in marine and luxury watercraft.

For our first public appearance in the U.S. market, we brought the IWAVE Wavekart S — the world's first electric water go-kart — to Miami's iconic waters. The response? Overwhelming excitement, smiles, and a steady stream of inquiries from luxury resort operators, yacht owners, and watersports enthusiasts.

What makes the IWAVE Wavekart stand out

  • 01
    Intuitive "hop in & go" designNo license required, just press the foot throttle and experience pure fun.
  • 02
    Zero emissionsAdvanced electric drive system. No engine noise. No fumes. An eco-friendly ride.
  • 03
    Drift & race on waterSpeeds up to 50 km/h (31 mph), precision steering, and agile handling — the thrill of racing, on the sea.
  • 04
    Modular & customizableBuilt for private and commercial markets. Options for branding and easy fleet management.

Throughout the five-day event we met with a wide range of partners — from top U.S. and European dealers to luxury hotel groups and adventure fleet operators. Test rides drew a diverse audience: families seeking new ways to enjoy the water, adventure lovers looking for the next big thrill, and marina managers exploring premium new offerings for their clients.

What's next

Following the show, IWAVE is expanding its dealer network in Florida and the U.S. East Coast, preparing for resort and private fleet deliveries this summer, and working on next-generation product development.

Thank you to everyone who visited us — your enthusiasm and ideas inspire us to keep pushing the boundaries of what's possible on water.

Stay tuned for more updates as IWAVE rides the next wave of innovation. To talk fleet or test rides, reach out at info@iwavekart.com.
