ANNOUNCEMENT · June 13, 2025

IWAVE launches its first product — Wavekart S redefines luxury watersports.

SHARE Twitter LinkedIn Email

Today we're thrilled to announce the global launch of IWAVE's first product: the Wavekart S — the world's first electric water go-kart.

With this launch we aren't just introducing a new product; we're creating a new way to experience the water.

A new category: water go-kart

The vision behind IWAVE is simple — reimagine the future of watersports.

Jet skis have dominated the market for decades, but they come with limits: noise, fuel, safety concerns, and licensing requirements. We believed there was a better way.

Wavekart S is our answer. An intuitive, fully electric watercraft that brings the thrill of go-karting to the water.

Designed for riders of all ages and skill levels, it combines effortless control with high performance and luxury design.

Our mission — redefining watersports for the next generation

At IWAVE we believe the future of watersports must be:

  • Electric & sustainable — our oceans deserve it.
  • Safe & accessible — for everyone, from first-timers to experienced riders.
  • Fun & stylish — a true luxury toy, built to delight.
  • Community-driven — built and improved together with our riders.

What's next

We're expanding our dealer network in the U.S. and Europe, with first resort and private deliveries shipping this summer. The Wavekart X — our Family Edition — follows, designed for fleets and shared shorelines.

If you'd like to be among the first to ride, reach out at info@iwavekart.com.
